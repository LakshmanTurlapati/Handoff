## Verification (must_haves)

  Verified by `apps/web/app/launch/[publicId]/route.ts` + `apps/web/tests/unit/handoff-launch-page.test.ts`.

- Expired, revoked, or unknown launch ids fail closed.

## Deviations from PLAN

- Launch entrypoint implemented as `apps/web/app/launch/[publicId]/route.ts` (Route Handler) instead of the planned `page.tsx`. Required because the device-session cookie must be written before any render, which a Route Handler with a 302 redirect is the standard Next.js shape for.
