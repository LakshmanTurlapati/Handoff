- BRAILLE_BASE = 0x2800

- Right column dots (top to bottom): 4, 5, 6, 8 → bits 0x08, 0x10, 0x20, 0x80

- sparklineFromRing(ring: Float32Array, writeIndex: number): walk writeIndex+1..writeIndex modulo 80, pair samples (2i, 2i+1) into 40 cells, intensity = sample * 4 clamped to 0..4
